# Hourly Income Calculator

A simple web-based calculator that helps freelancers and job seekers estimate their earnings.

## Features

- Enter hourly rate ($)
- Custom hours per week
- Automatic weekly income calculation
- Automatic monthly income calculation
- Automatic yearly income calculation
- Dark Mode / Light Mode
- Mobile-friendly design

## Usage

1. Enter your hourly rate.
2. Enter hours worked per week.
3. View weekly, monthly, and yearly earnings instantly.
